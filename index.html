01 Equity Day-Trade Scanner Quantitative Systems Python · pandas · SQLite Testing ›

EQDT-v1 scans a dynamic universe of 100+ equities each morning for high-conviction long day-trade setups. A hard-gate layer first filters on daily ADX (trend quality vs. chop), relative volume pace versus the 20-day average, dollar liquidity, and ATR-based volatility range. Tickers clearing those gates run through eight independent scoring agents covering opening range breakout, VWAP positioning, 5-minute RSI momentum, EMA trend structure (9/21 stack), gap behavior at open, daily moving average context, and intraday range position, modeled on the multi-agent consensus architecture in MAMS-v4. Setups scoring ≥70 composite conviction with ≥5/8 agents bullish are logged to SQLite with a defined entry, 1.5× ATR stop, and 2R target. After the close, the system simulates each trade on 5-minute bar data and grades the outcome: stop, target, or 15:50 ET time exit. Push alerts delivered via ntfy.sh at scan and grade time.

02 ETH Swing Trading System Algorithmic Trading Pine Script v6 · TradingView Live ›

MAMS-v4 is a Pine Script v6 swing trading system for ETH/USD on the 1-hour timeframe, currently running live. Thirteen independent signal agents (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ADX, ATR-normalized momentum, and several volume-weighted indicators) vote on market direction; only trades where consensus clears a conviction threshold are taken. A regime detection layer identifies high-trend versus high-chop environments and suppresses signals when directional quality is low, reducing exposure during whipsaw conditions. Position sizing scales with realized volatility, and trailing stops adapt to ATR to lock in gains during extended moves while respecting market structure.

03 Prediction Market Arb Monitor Market Microstructure Python · Kalshi API Development ›

A research and monitoring system for Kalshi prediction markets built to detect informational lag between Kalshi-implied probabilities and their reference instruments. The system scans active markets and filters for situations where market prices appear to diverge from correlated reference data: spot crypto prices, scheduled Fed communications, economic releases. When divergence is detected, it dispatches parallel AI research agents to synthesize primary sources (Fed minutes, legislative text, economic data) at a depth that manual review wouldn't sustain. Each analysis produces a structured recommendation (TRADE / PASS / NEEDS MORE WORK) with a cited rationale. Kalshi markets tend to reprice more slowly than the underlying instruments they reference, and the system is designed to systematically identify those windows before they close.
